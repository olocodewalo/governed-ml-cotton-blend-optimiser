# Imperfections: neps, thick places, thin places

"Imperfections" (IPI) is the count per 1000 m of thin places (-50%), thick
places (+50%), and **neps** (+200%). Neps usually dominate.

- **Neps** are tight tangles of fibre. They form from **immature (low
  micronaire) fibre**, from **high short-fibre content**, from mechanical
  over-processing, and from trash. Immaturity and SFC interact -- a blend high
  in both is much worse than the sum.
- Neps survive spinning and appear as slubs in fabric and as dyeing faults
  (immature fibre under-dyes, so a nep shows as a light speck).
- Combing removes neps and short fibre together, at a noil cost.

The quality model predicts total imperfections from a **neps proxy** that is
super-linear in SFC and in micronaire distance from ~4.0, scaled by micronaire
spread. The count spec sets a maximum.
