# Staple length

Staple length (upper-half mean length, mm) is the single strongest lever on
spinnability and yarn strength. Longer fibres:

- overlap more in the yarn, so more fibres carry load at any cross-section ->
  higher **CSP** and tenacity;
- need less twist for the same strength, giving a softer yarn and higher
  productivity;
- reduce **ends-down** (spinning breaks) because the yarn tolerates weak spots
  better.

Rule of thumb: each additional 1 mm of blend staple length is worth a
meaningful CSP gain and lets the mill spin ~2-3 counts finer. Longer staple
cotton (Pima, Giza) is priced at a premium for exactly this reason. The
trade-off the optimiser manages is buying just enough length to clear the count
spec without over-paying.
