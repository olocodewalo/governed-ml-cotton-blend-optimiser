# Fibre strength (g/tex)

Bundle strength measured on the HVI at 1/8" gauge, in grams per tex. Upland
cotton runs ~26-32 g/tex; ELS cottons exceed 34.

- Fibre strength translates fairly directly into **yarn tenacity and CSP**.
- It matters most for **warp yarn** and for high-speed weaving/knitting where
  ends-down and stops kill productivity.
- Strength and **short-fibre content** interact: a strong fibre with high SFC
  still spins a weak, hairy yarn because the short fibres do not anchor.

In blending, weighted-average strength is kept within a tight band of the
rolling average (e.g. +/- 1.5 g/tex) so that yarn strength - and loom
performance - does not swing week to week.
