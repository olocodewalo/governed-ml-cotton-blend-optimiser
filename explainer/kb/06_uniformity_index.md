# Length uniformity index

Uniformity index (%) is the ratio of mean length to upper-half mean length. It
describes how *consistent* the fibre lengths are, and it moves inversely with
short-fibre content.

- Typical values 78-85%. Above ~82% is good.
- Low uniformity means a wide length distribution -> irregular drafting ->
  higher **U%** and weak places in the yarn.
- Uniformity is a useful cross-check on SFC: if a lab only reports HVI, low
  uniformity is the warning sign that AFIS would show high SFC.

The optimiser treats uniformity as a secondary quality driver: helpful for
evenness and CSP, but staple length and SFC dominate.
