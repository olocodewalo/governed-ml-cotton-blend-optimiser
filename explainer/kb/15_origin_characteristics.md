# Cotton by origin (typical character)

Broad tendencies only -- every lot must be tested.

- **India Shankar-6 (Gujarat/MP)**: medium staple (28-30 mm), micronaire
  4.0-4.8, moderate strength, higher trash from machine picking, competitively
  priced. The volume workhorse for 20s-40s.
- **US Pima (SJV)**: extra-long staple (>34 mm as grown), high strength, low
  SFC, very clean, machine-picked. Premium; used to lift a blend for fine or
  strong yarn.
- **West African (Francophone, hand-picked)**: medium staple, good colour, but
  **contamination risk** (polypropylene, hair) and variable maturity. Priced at
  a small discount; share is usually capped.
- **Egyptian Giza**: long staple, high strength and uniformity, low trash,
  excellent for combed fine counts. Premium and supply-constrained.

Blending across origins manages cost, but contamination and colour risk travel
with even a small share, so origin fractions are watched as well as fibre
numbers.
