# Trash and contamination

**Trash** (leaf, bark, seed-coat fragments, dust) is reported as a percentage or
a trash-area grade. It:

- forces more aggressive cleaning at blow-room and card, which itself breaks
  fibre and raises SFC;
- passes through as **specks and imperfections** in the yarn;
- raises the **U%** slightly and depresses spinning efficiency.

**Contamination** (polypropylene, jute, hair, coloured fibre) is different: it
cannot be cleaned out and shows up as a fault in the finished fabric, sometimes
after dyeing. Contamination risk is strongly **origin-linked** -- hand-picked
cottons and certain regions carry more foreign matter. Blending a small
fraction of a high-risk origin still injects its contamination across the whole
production run, so mixing masters cap the share of suspect origins regardless of
price.
