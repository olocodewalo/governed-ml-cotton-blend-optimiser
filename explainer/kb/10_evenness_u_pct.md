# Yarn evenness (U% and CV%)

U% (or CVm%) from a capacitive evenness tester measures the **mass variation
along the yarn**. Lower is better. CV% ~= 1.25 x U%.

Drivers:

- **Short-fibre content** and poor length uniformity -> drafting waves.
- **Micronaire spread** across the blend -> fibres of unlike fineness draft
  unevenly.
- Trash and neps -> local mass spikes.
- Mechanical: worn drafting aprons, wrong break-draft, roller slip (out of scope
  for the blend model but part of real ends-down).

Uneven yarn shows as streaks and barre in knitted fabric and weak spots in
woven fabric. Spec sheets give a **maximum U%** per count; finer counts demand
lower U%. In this project U% is a non-linear function of SFC and micronaire
variance, which is why a linear cost rule cannot manage it.
