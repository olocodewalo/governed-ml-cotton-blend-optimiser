# Bale laydown / mixing practice

A **laydown** is the set of bales opened together to feed the blow-room for a
production lot -- typically 30-60 bales.

Good practice:

- **Many bales, small share each.** Spreading the blend over more bales dilutes
  the effect of any one off-spec bale and smooths the profile. A blend that
  leans on 3-4 dominant bales is fragile.
- **Spread each property.** Don't put all the low-micronaire bales adjacent in
  the laydown sequence; interleave so the card sees an average.
- **Category mixing.** Group bales into micronaire/strength categories and draw
  proportionally from each.
- **Hold a reserve.** Keep some mid-grade bales uncommitted to correct the next
  laydown toward the long-term average.

In the optimiser this is captured by a maximum-share-per-bale cap, a minimum and
maximum bale count, and the long-term-blend drift limits.
